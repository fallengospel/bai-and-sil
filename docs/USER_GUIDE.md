## 1. Welcome to BAI & SIL

### What is BAI & SIL?

BAI & SIL is a Filipino online marketplace where you can buy and sell pre-loved items. The name represents the buyer-seller relationship in Filipino culture.

### Why Use BAI & SIL?

- **Free to list** — No fees for sellers
- **Local focus** — Meet-up transactions in your area
- **Filipino-made** — Built for Filipinos, by Filipinos
- **Safe & secure** — Verified users and secure messaging
- **Easy to use** — Simple, clean interface

### Quick Start

1. Visit https://bai-and-sil.vercel.app
2. Click "Register"
3. Choose your role (Buyer or Seller)
4. Start browsing or listing items!

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Creating an Account

**Step 1:** Click "Register" in the top right corner

**Step 2:** Fill in your details:
- Full Name
- Email Address
- Password (minimum 6 characters)
- Phone Number (optional)

**Step 3:** Choose your role:
- **Buyer** — Browse and purchase items
- **Seller** — List and sell items

**Step 4:** Select your location

**Step 5:** Click "Create Account"

You're automatically logged in after registration!

### 2.2 Logging In

**Step 1:** Click "Login" in the top right corner

**Step 2:** Enter your email and password

**Step 3:** Click "Log In"

You'll be redirected to your dashboard based on your role.

### 2.3 Your Dashboard

After logging in, you'll see your personalized dashboard:

- **Buyer Dashboard** — Browse items, view favorites, see recommendations
- **Seller Dashboard** — Manage listings, view stats, create new listings
- **Admin Dashboard** — Manage users, listings, and reports

## 3. For Buyers

### 3.1 Browsing Items

**Home Page:**
- See the latest "Bagong Items" (New Items)
- Browse by category
- View "Paano Gamitin?" (How to Use) guide

**Categories Page:**
- 12 categories to choose from:
  - 💻 Electronics
  - 👗 Fashion
  - 🏠 Home & Living
  - 🚗 Vehicles
  - 💄 Beauty
  - ⚽ Sports
  - 🎮 Gaming
  - 🏆 Collectibles
  - 🔌 Appliances
  - 🔧 Tools
  - 📚 Books
  - 📦 Other

### 3.2 Searching for Items

**Using the Search Bar:**
1. Type what you're looking for
2. Press Enter or click the search icon
3. View results

**Using Filters:**
1. Go to the Search page
2. Use filters on the left:
   - Category
   - Price range (min/max)
   - Condition (Brand New, Like New, Good, Fair)
   - Location
3. Sort results by:
   - Newest
   - Price: Low to High
   - Price: High to Low

### 3.3 Viewing an Item

Click on any listing to see:
- Full description
- Multiple photos
- Price in Philippine Peso (₱)
- Seller information
- Location
- Similar items

### 3.4 Saving to Favorites

1. Click the heart icon on any listing
2. The heart turns red when saved
3. View all favorites in "Favorites" page

### 3.5 Messaging a Seller

1. Go to the listing detail page
2. Click "Message Seller"
3. Type your message
4. Press Enter to send

**Tips:**
- Ask about the item's condition
- Negotiate the price
- Arrange a meet-up location and time

### 3.6 Making an Offer

1. Go to the listing detail page
2. Click "Make Offer"
3. Enter your offer amount (in ₱)
4. Add an optional message
5. Click "Send Offer"

The seller will be notified and can accept or decline.

### 3.7 Leaving a Review

After a transaction:
1. Go to the seller's profile
2. Click "Leave Review"
3. Rate 1-5 stars
4. Write a comment (optional)
5. Submit

## 4. For Sellers

### 4.1 Creating a Listing

**Step 1:** Click "Sell" in the navbar

**Step 2:** Fill in Item Details:
- Title (be specific, e.g., "iPhone 14 Pro 256GB Space Black")
- Description (include condition, age, reason for selling)
- Condition (Brand New, Like New, Good, Fair)
- Category

**Step 3:** Set Pricing:
- Price in Philippine Peso (₱)
- Location (city/area)

**Step 4:** Add Photos:
- Click "Upload Images"
- Select up to 5 photos
- Drag to reorder
- First photo is the main image

**Step 5:** Review and Publish:
- Check all details
- Click "Publish Listing"

**Daily Limit:** 20 listings per day

### 4.2 Managing Your Listings

**My Listings Page:**
- View all your listings
- Filter by status (Active, Sold, Reserved)
- Edit or delete listings

**Edit Listing:**
1. Go to My Listings
2. Click "Edit" on a listing
3. Make changes
4. Save

**Mark as Sold:**
1. Go to My Listings
2. Click "Mark as Sold"
3. Confirm

### 4.3 Responding to Messages

1. Go to Messages
2. Click on a conversation
3. Read the buyer's message
4. Type your response
5. Press Enter to send

### 4.4 Handling Offers

1. Go to "My Offers"
2. View pending offers
3. Click "Accept" or "Decline"
4. Add an optional message

**Tips:**
- Respond quickly to offers
- Counter-offer if needed
- Be polite and professional

### 4.5 Seller Dashboard

Your dashboard shows:
- Total listings
- Active listings
- Items sold
- Total views
- Average views per listing
- Conversion rate

**Quick Actions:**
- Create new listing
- Export listings to CSV
- View messages

## 8. Safety Tips

### 8.1 For All Users

- **Verify before meeting** — Check the item in person
- **Meet in public** — Use safe meeting spots (malls, police stations)
- **Bring a friend** — Don't meet alone
- **Trust your instincts** — If it feels wrong, walk away
- **Don't share personal info** — Keep home address private

### 8.2 For Buyers

- **Inspect the item** — Check for damage before paying
- **Negotiate fairly** — Don't lowball sellers
- **Pay securely** — Use GCash/Maya when possible
- **Get proof** — Screenshot the transaction
- **Leave honest reviews** — Help other buyers

### 8.3 For Sellers

- **Describe accurately** — Don't hide defects
- **Take clear photos** — Show all angles
- **Respond quickly** — Buyers appreciate fast replies
- **Be professional** — Stay polite even if declined
- **Keep records** — Screenshot conversations

### 8.4 Reporting Issues

**To report a listing:**
1. Go to the listing
2. Click "Report"
3. Select a reason
4. Add details
5. Submit

**To report a user:**
1. Go to their profile
2. Click "Report"
3. Select a reason
4. Add details
5. Submit

## 9. FAQ

### General

**Q: Is BAI & SIL free?**
A: Yes! It's free to register, list items, and browse. No hidden fees.

**Q: How do I contact support?**
A: Email us at support@baiandsil.ph

**Q: Can I use BAI & SIL on my phone?**
A: Yes! The website is mobile-friendly and works on all devices.

### Buying

**Q: How do I pay?**
A: Arrange payment directly with the seller. Common methods: GCash, Maya, cash on meet-up.

**Q: Can I return an item?**
A: Returns depend on the seller's policy. Discuss before purchasing.

**Q: What if I get scammed?**
A: Report the user immediately. We'll investigate and take action.

### Selling

**Q: How many items can I list?**
A: Up to 20 items per day.

**Q: Can I edit my listing?**
A: Yes! Go to My Listings and click Edit.

**Q: How do I mark an item as sold?**
A: Go to My Listings and click "Mark as Sold."

### Account

**Q: How do I change my password?**
A: Go to Profile → Edit Profile → Change Password.

**Q: Can I delete my account?**
A: Contact support at support@baiandsil.ph

**Q: How do I become a verified seller?**
A: Submit your government ID through the verification page. Admins will review within 24 hours.
